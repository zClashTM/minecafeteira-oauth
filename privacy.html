Privacy Policy

MINECAFETEIRA is a private application used by authorized administrators to manage backups through Google Drive.

Google Drive access is used only for authorized backup operations.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally by rclone on the authorized computer.

MINECAFETEIRA does not sell or intentionally share Google user data with third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time through the user's Google Account.
